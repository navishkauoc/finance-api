# Requirements / Story

1. Customers need to register first in the system.\
   1.1. Once a Customer is registered, they will belong to CUSTOMER role.\
   1.2. They should enter their details including name, mobile number, and email in order to receive notifications.
2. Customers will receive invoices with due dates.\
   2.1. Invoices will include line items with more details.
3. Finance Officer will generate invoices for Customers.\
   3.1. If the Customer is not already in the system, Finance Officer will register a customer before invoicing them.\
   3.2. A customer who is registered by a Finance Officer will receive email and WhatsApp notifications regarding their registration and other user actions (ex: invoice, payment receipt, or credit note generation).
4. Invoices generated by Finance Officer will go through an approval workflow.
5. Finance Admins will review the Invoices generated by Finance Officer, and they will perform actions like, Approve or Reject.\
   5.1. Invoices will be rejected with a reason. Finance Officer can check this reason and re-submit with a reply or cancel that invoice.\
   5.2. Invoices that are pending for approvals will not be eligible for payments.
